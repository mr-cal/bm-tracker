How this works

Log it as it happens

A day counts if you record it on the day it happened, in your own timezone. There is no grace period and no way to waive a missed day — the whole game is opening the app at the moment it happens.

Nothing is a valid answer

You do not have to have pooped. Recording "nothing today" is a real entry: it counts, it keeps your streak, and it can carry a note. "Long flight, nothing happened" is a better diary entry than no entry at all.

+{{ constants.backfill_points }}, but it does not join your streak
An achievement
{{ constants.tier_points }}, by how rare it is

The streak bonus stops climbing at day six, on purpose. A long run should be worth something without one missed day becoming a debt you never clear.

A worked day

  • Monday. Your first of a run: {{ constants.day_points }} points.
  • Tuesday. Second day, so {{ constants.day_points }} + 1.
  • Wednesday. Third day, {{ constants.day_points }} + 2, and you wrote a note, so +{{ constants.note_points }} more.
  • Thursday. You ate a curry and the note says so. Fourth day, {{ constants.day_points }} + 3.
  • Friday. Fifth day: {{ constants.day_points }} + 4. From here the bonus stops moving.

So five days in a row is {{ worked_total }} points, and a week is {{ week_total }}. A whole year of perfect logging is about {{ year_total }} points.

A new year starts over

Streaks stop at 31 December and achievements re-earn every January, so everyone competes over the same window and nobody is permanently behind because they joined in March.

What everyone can see

Other people in the group can see your notes, streak, points and achievements. They cannot see the time of day of a BM, its Bristol type as an individual event, or which days you filled in late — only you and an admin can.

Achievements

{{ constants.achievement_count }} of them so far, all listed from the start whether you have earned them or not — most are unlikely and the absurdity is the point. Each one is worth {{ constants.tier_min }} to {{ constants.tier_max }} points depending on how hard it is, and they count toward your leaderboard total like anything else you earn. Achievements reset every January, so last year's are last year's.

Some are found by reading your notes

{{ constants.note_achievement_count }} of them are not counts at all. They are found by what a note said — a poem, a complaint about work, a night you did not sleep through, a bereavement. Every note you write is compared against them, so there is nothing to do about them and no progress towards them: either a note says the thing, or it does not. Nothing happens if you write nothing, and writing a lot is not the way to collect them either.

They are worth the same points as the counted ones and they count the same on the board, and they reset every January like everything else.
